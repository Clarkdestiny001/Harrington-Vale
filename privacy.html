Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Harrington & Vale ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and safeguards personal information when you visit our website or submit an enquiry through our contact form.

Information We Collect

When you submit a consultation request, we collect the information you provide directly, which may include your name, email address, phone number, company, matter type, and any message you include. We do not knowingly collect information from you through any other means on this site.

How We Use Information

Information submitted through our contact form is used solely to respond to your enquiry, assess potential conflicts of interest, and, where appropriate, follow up regarding legal services. We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

Data Retention

We retain enquiry information only for as long as necessary to respond to your request and comply with applicable professional and legal obligations, after which it is securely deleted.

Confidentiality

All enquiries are treated with strict confidentiality. Submitting a message through our contact form does not, on its own, create an attorney-client relationship.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to enquiries@harringtonvale.com.
